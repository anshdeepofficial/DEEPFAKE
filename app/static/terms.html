Terms & responsible use

DeepGuard provides investigative signals, not legal proof.

Built-in media scores are heuristic and can produce false positives and false negatives. Claim verification depends on available public evidence and can remain incomplete or contradictory.

Do not use a score as the sole basis for harm

Do not use DeepGuard alone to accuse a person of fraud, impose discipline, make legal findings, deny services, or take other high-impact action. Important conclusions require human review and primary-source verification.

Content rights

Only upload or analyse content you are allowed to process. You remain responsible for copyright, privacy, confidentiality and other rights connected to submitted media or text.

Availability

Public search providers, fact-check databases and optional external model services may change, rate-limit or become unavailable. An unavailable provider must not be interpreted as evidence that a claim is true or false.
